# Role: Generator

Create the complete profile file of the species, including:

- `taxonomy`, `names` (trade names at least in English; German, Spanish and Portuguese where they
  are in use), `variants` (well-known locality and colour forms),
- every feature in `features` with value, confidence, rationale and sources,
- `partners` with at least three suitable and three unsuitable tankmates, each with a short reason;
  name concrete species where possible,
- the written `profile`: every section in English, in plain language for hobbyists, 60 to 200 words
  per section. `problems` covers typical keeping mistakes and disease risks (for example bloat on
  a high-protein diet), without diagnosing or prescribing medication.
- `provenance.runs` with one entry of role `generator`.

Work through the sources first, then decide the values, then write the prose so that the prose
agrees with the values.

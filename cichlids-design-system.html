Design System

Extracted from the finished, validated tank prototype — including the building blocks from onboarding, the "create tank" wizard and tank settings. A restrained, border-led language with a single aquatic accent. v0.3 captures foundations and components; machine-readable tokens (light + dark) live in design-system/tokens.css and tokens.json.

Foundations

Motion

Three durations, two easings — that's the whole budget. Animate opacity and transform only (never layout). Motion confirms an action or guides attention; it is never decorative. prefers-reduced-motion is honored everywhere.

Toggles, hovers, chip selection120ms
Most transitions, sheets, reveals200ms
Screen / player transitions280ms

Easing: --ease-standard cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) for most; --ease-emphasized cubic-bezier(.3,.8,.3,1) for sheets & large surfaces.

Foundations

Accessibility

Target is WCAG AA in both themes — binding, not aspirational.

  • Contrast: body text (--fg) and the primary-button pairing (--on-accent on --accent) meet AA in light and dark. --muted is for secondary text only and must not drop below AA at its size; never use it for primary content.
  • Color is never the only signal: status and errors always pair color with an icon and/or text (see warning card & field error).
  • Focus: every interactive element shows a visible ring on keyboard navigation via :focus-visible (--focus-ring).
  • Hit areas: minimum 48dp; icon buttons are 44–48px even when the glyph is smaller.
  • Motion: honors prefers-reduced-motion; nothing essential depends on animation.
